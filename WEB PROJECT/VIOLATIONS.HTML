Traffic E-Ticketing System

As Addis Ababa continues to grow rapidly, daily rush hour congestion along major corridors—such as Bole Road, Meskel Square, Megenagna, and the Ring Road—puts immense pressure on the city's road network. During peak morning and evening commutes, heavy traffic jams often trigger impulsive driving behaviors, including illegal lane cutting, red-light running, speeding in clear stretches, and unauthorized roadside parking that blocks public transport and emergency lanes. Traditional paper-based enforcement struggles to keep up with this volume and leaves room for inconsistent reporting, which is why our Traffic E-Ticketing System logs every infraction digitally with clear timestamps, standardized fine amounts, and verifiable payment tracking to promote fairer, safer streets across the capital.
